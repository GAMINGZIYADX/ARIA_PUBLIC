# Code of Conduct

ARIA is a project created by **Ziyad Noucair**. This Code of Conduct applies to
everyone participating in the ARIA community — issues, pull requests, discussions,
and any other project space.

---

## 1. Expected behavior

Everyone taking part in this project is expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and discussions
- Accept feedback gracefully, and give it kindly
- Assume good faith, especially across language and experience gaps
- Give proper credit when building on the work of others
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest when contributing

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory or demeaning comments about anyone's identity or background
- Unwelcome sexual attention, or sexualized language and imagery
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions
- Introducing intentionally harmful, backdoored, or malicious code

---

## 2. Attribution and honesty

ARIA is released under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE), which permits forks,
modifications, and derivative works — including commercial ones. The license does
require that you:

- Retain the copyright notice and the [NOTICE](NOTICE) file in redistributions
- State any significant changes you made to the code

Beyond those legal requirements, the community expectation is simple: **be honest
about where the code came from.** Do not misrepresent ARIA's origin, authorship,
or capabilities, and do not present a lightly-modified fork as wholly original
work. Building on ARIA is encouraged; obscuring that you did is not.

---

## 3. Responsible use

The Apache 2.0 license does not restrict how you may use this software, and this
section does not attempt to add legal restrictions to it. It states what this
project's community considers acceptable, and what the maintainer will support.

ARIA is a local assistant with access to a microphone, the filesystem, and shell
execution. Please do not use it for:

- Surveillance, tracking, or monitoring people without their knowledge and consent
- Building spyware, keyloggers, or other malicious automation
- Deceptively impersonating real people or organizations
- Generating or distributing illegal content
- Anything that violates applicable law

Requests for help with the above will be declined, and contributions in that
direction will not be merged.

## 5. Reporting and enforcement

Report violations of this Code of Conduct privately to **znoucair@gmail.com**.

Reports are handled confidentially. Please include what happened, where, and any
links or context that help — you will get an acknowledgement, and the reporter's
identity will not be shared with the reported party without permission.

ARIA is currently maintained by one person, who reviews reports directly. If a
report concerns the maintainer, say so and it will be handled as directly and
fairly as possible.

Responses are proportionate to the behavior, and generally escalate:

1. A private warning explaining what was wrong
2. A temporary block from participation
3. A permanent block, and removal of contributions where warranted

Serious violations — harassment, malicious code, deliberate misrepresentation —
may skip straight to a permanent block.

> **Security vulnerabilities are not Code of Conduct reports.** Report those
> privately as described in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#reporting-security-issues),
> and do not open a public issue.
